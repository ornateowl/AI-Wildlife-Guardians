Endangered Species from Deforestation

Many animals are going extinct due to the cutting down of trees, causing habitat loss. These include:

Chimpanzee

Giant Panda

Harpy Eagle

Javan Hawk-Eagle

Forest Owlet

All of these animals are at risk of extinction because of habitat loss from deforestation. Deforestation is usually done to accommodate urban expansion and agricultural activities. Thirty years of deforestation in the Amazon Rainforest resulted in the extinction of 10 mammal species, 20 bird species, and 8 amphibian species. If we continue to put our interests before those of the wildlife that we coexist with, whole ecosystems can be disrupted. Our AI camera combats these threats by detecting unauthorized deforestation (like heavy logging equipment detected in protected or restricted zones) and helping protect vulnerable wildlife from harm.
