The Issue

For centuries, humans have been abusing the resources of our planet for our own self-serving benefit. In this process, we ruin ecosystems and put them into imbalance. Many species have been threatened with endangerment and even have gone extinct.

We hope to solve the problem of unjust treatment toward many animal species due to human greed for resources. We deeply value the concept of environmental justice, which states that all people should be protected equally from environmental hazards, and we hope to protect animals in the same way. Poaching and deforestation not only drive species to extinction but also disrupt entire ecosystems, disproportionately harming endangered animals that have no protection or voice. Our AI prototype aims to fix this unethical treatment toward wildlife, where a webcam and computer vision work together to detect suspicious activity and protect animals.
