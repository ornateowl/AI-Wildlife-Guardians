AI Wildlife Guardians

Our Mission

At AI Wildlife Guardians, we aim to protect species from endangerment and extinction through AI and computer vision-enhanced webcams. This main website provides info on animal extinction, and it includes a sample of a camera that utilizes AI and computer vision to detect suspicious activity like poaching or deforestation in protected areas. This website also provides information about why these illegal activities should be combatted, examples of deforestation and poaching's effect on animals, and future iterations that plan for a sustainable future.
