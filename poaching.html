Endangered Species from Poaching

Many animals are going extinct due to illegal hunting and trafficking. These include:

Antelope

Buffalo

Rhino

Elephant

African Grey Parrot

It is important to note that poaching does not only refer to the illegal hunting of animals; it can also refer to the trapping of wild animals to illegally trade them, which is the case for African Gray Parrots. Other animals are illegally hunted for their meat, tusks, horns, or other body parts. According to National Geographic, poaching is the primary cause of extinction for some animals. Despite being protected by law, these animals are still harmed, and our AI camera tries to fix this issue.
