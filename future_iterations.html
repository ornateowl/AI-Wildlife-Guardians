Future Iterations + Impact

As of now, the computer vision webcam detects and labels objects in real time. In the future, we plan to implement an automated alert system that notifies nearby park rangers and conservationists through a dedicated app whenever weapons, such as guns or axes, are detected in protected areas. This will enable faster responses to illegal poaching and deforestation, helping to safeguard endangered wildlife.

Our long-term goal is to transform this project into a global conservation tool, using AI to monitor vast ecosystems 24/7 without disrupting natural habitats. The system could be powered by solar energy, ensuring sustainable operation in remote locations. Additionally, we envision:

- AI-Powered data analysis to track patterns of illegal activity to predict and prevent future threats.

- Wildlife monitoring, where detection is expanded to identify, count, and record locations of endangered species, ensuring their protection.

Traditionally, these AI cameras have been used to detect threats around houses as security cameras. We made our solution unique by protecting wildlife through the prevention of illegal poaching and deforestation. Therefore, this project aims to be more than just a security tool-- it envisions a future where technology and sustainability intersect to protect ecosystems and promote a healthier planet for generations to come. By focusing on prevention and preservation, this AI solution contributes to the long-term fight against the extinction of vulnerable species.
